# PILOT_REPORT

Self-tests for the Dialing for Videos campaign (McGrady et al. 2023). Generated by `python -m sampler.pilot report`.

## Summary of findings

- **OR syntax and case-insensitivity work** (test 1). `OR` and `|` are equivalent; `OR` is kept as the paper's operator.
- **YouTube searches only the first 31 OR'ed IDs.** The 32nd and later are ignored, independent of query length (test 2: last-position known ID found at 16/24/30/31 IDs, never at 32/33/40). A 32-ID query therefore covers 31 x 1024 IDs, not 32,768. The campaign still sends 32 IDs as published, records each hit's position (`queried_pos`; a hit at position 31 would mean the cap changed), and reports two estimates: the paper method (32) and a cap-corrected one (31), which is 32/31 = 3.2% higher. Whether the cap existed in 2022 cannot be checked from here.
- **Known-hit recall: 90/100 at random positions.** Of the 10 misses, 3 were at position 31 (the cap above), 5 were known IDs containing `_` or `--` (the old prefix sample's IDs all contain a dash; campaign IDs are letters only), and 2 are unexplained. In the position scan, IDs at positions 22-30 were found 53/54 times (both separators).
- **Empty result pages are a throttling signal.** In the local throughput run they were 25% of attempts, alongside HTTP 403s, at only 7 qps from one home IP. Empty pages are retried, and a query still empty after retries is excluded from the denominator rather than counted as zero hits.
- A bug found and fixed while running test 1: the hit validator required position 11 to match case-exactly, which rejected ~half of the known IDs (sent with flipped case). Earlier runs are kept as `pilot/known_hit_run*_*.json` for the record.

## 4. Mock backend

`tests/mock_server.py` serves the InnerTube search endpoint with a planted hit rate (an ID exists iff `sha1(id.lower()) % hit_den == 0`, returned in a hash-derived case), decoy results, and injected 429/500 errors. The unit and integration tests (`pytest -q`) run only against it.
